# Sarkari-Saathi – Solution Pitch

**Project by:** Tanisha Kapoor

**Challenge:** The Great Indian Internship Challenge – Gnani AI

## 1. Problem Statement

Many Indian citizens face difficulties discovering government schemes relevant to their needs. Language barriers and complicated information can make accessing information about government benefits challenging.

## 2. Proposed Solution

Sarkari-Saathi is a voice-first AI-powered assistant designed to help Indian citizens discover relevant government schemes through simple conversations in Hindi or English.

It makes scheme discovery more conversational and accessible using speech recognition, AI-powered matching, and voice responses.

## 3. Key Features

- **Voice Interaction:** Users can ask questions using their microphone in Hindi or English.
- **Speech-to-Text:** Gnani Prisma converts spoken queries into text.
- **AI Scheme Matching:** Google Gemini identifies potentially relevant schemes from the available scheme dataset.
- **Text-to-Speech:** Gnani Timbre generates voice responses.
- **Scheme Cards:** Relevant scheme information is displayed through the application's interface.

## 4. Technology Stack

- Python
- FastAPI
- Gnani Prisma – Speech-to-Text
- Gnani Timbre – Text-to-Speech
- Google Gemini AI

## 5. How It Works

1. The user speaks a query through the application's microphone.
2. Gnani Prisma converts the speech into text.
3. Google Gemini processes the query and matches it with relevant schemes from the available dataset.
4. Gnani Timbre generates a spoken response.
5. The application presents the response along with relevant scheme cards.

## 6. Expected Impact

Sarkari-Saathi aims to simplify government scheme discovery by reducing language and accessibility barriers. Its voice-first approach provides a convenient way for citizens to explore information about potentially relevant government schemes.
